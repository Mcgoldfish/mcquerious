# The Title Race Lab / 英超冠军实验室

DS/Maths route #/experiments/title-lab. Interactive probability, conditional-match radar and replayable parallel universes.
2023–24 club membership; generated circle-method double round robin, not actual historical fixtures. All start at zero points.
Strengths are manual illustrative constants, not empirical fit or current forecast. Selected strength multiplier and home advantage configurable.
Independent Poisson goals: home mean 1.32 * strength ratio * home advantage; away mean 1.32 / ratio.
3/1/0 points. Final sort points, GD, GF, then seeded random playoff surrogate; full official head-to-head tie provisions not implemented.
No injuries, punishments, transfers or dynamic form. Simplified assumptions are disclosed in page.
Seeded seasons and per-match streams. 1000/5000 simulations yield every 50 seasons to avoid blocking the interface. Configuration disabled while running.
Wilson 95% interval is simulation sampling uncertainty only, not model uncertainty. Zero wins is not proof of impossibility.
Radar: all 38 focus-team fixtures, displays eight largest conditional-frequency spreads, conditional champion frequency within W/D/L strata. These are observational model-conditioned groups, not causal interventions. Displays stratum counts.
Replay: 39 points snapshots; all round scores. Intermediate tied points listed by fixed club id; final table uses final ranking rules.
Saved examples bounded: first 40, additional focus-team-winning/comeback examples until at least 12/8 are saved (first 40 may already include more). Example prevalence is not a probability estimate.
Actual aggregate counts for focus wins and final-round come-from-behind champions collected across all runs. A comeback means final champion trailed in points after round 37.
Source links: official Premier League format and 2023–24 club review. No network data fetching, backend or new dependencies.
